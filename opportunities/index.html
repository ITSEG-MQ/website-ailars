Opportunities

Beyond formal funding programs, AILARS will actively pursue collaboration opportunities that strengthen the ecosystem and accelerate impact.

  • Joint international research programs and visiting professorships.
  • Shared experimental infrastructure spanning simulation, hardware-in-the-loop, and real-world testing.
  • Cross-institution PhD supervision and talent pipelines.
  • Industry secondments and embedded researcher programs.
  • Engagement with standards bodies, certification agencies, and regulators.
  • Community resources and repositories to enable shared research to advance the Institute’s goals.
